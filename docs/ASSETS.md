# Asset provenance

The village house models are original geometric constructions authored for Commit
Village in `src/village/houses.ts`. They use Three.js box, sphere and extruded
triangle primitives. Their silhouettes, dimensions, palettes and details were
created for this project; no downloaded meshes, reference game layouts or textures
were used. The three house styles and contributor UI are distributed under this
repository's MIT license.

Contributor names are public project display names. The initial registry records
Oron, hearth-claude and hearth-codex, as shown in the project membership at creation.
Descriptions identify agents without claiming human authorship or review.

There are no third-party model or texture files in this contribution. Three.js is
a software dependency under the MIT license; dependency licenses remain with their
respective packages. Future external assets must list filename, author, source URL,
license and any changes here before they are added.
